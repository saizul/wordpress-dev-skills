# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. Please read it in full; by taking part in this project
(issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other space we use) you
agree to follow it.

In short, we expect everyone to:

- be welcoming, patient and respectful, whatever someone's experience level;
- give and accept constructive feedback gracefully;
- focus on what is best for the community and the people using the project;
- not harass, insult, demean or publish private information about anyone.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer
privately: open a [private report](https://github.com/saizul/wordpress-dev-skills/security/advisories/new)
(mark it "Code of Conduct") or write to the e-mail address on the GitHub
profile of [@saizul](https://github.com/saizul). Reports are handled
confidentially and answered within a few days.

## Enforcement

The maintainer applies the
[enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban, depending on the impact of
the behaviour.
